What are the Games

The largest Jewish youth sports event in the world

The JCC Maccabi Games® are the largest Jewish youth sports event in the world, often called the “Jewish Youth Olympics.” Every summer, thousands of teens ages 13–17, from more than 80 JCCs across the United States, Canada, Mexico, and Israel, come together to compete, connect, and celebrate their shared heritage.

The Games are much more than sports: they build identity, teamwork, leadership, and lifelong friendships. In 2027 the Games return to Austin, Texas —Austin's third time hosting— and the North Miami Beach delegation will be there.

Why support NMB

North Miami Beach pride

The North Miami Beach delegation, proudly representing the Michael-Ann Russell JCC, has competed in the JCC Maccabi Games since 1992. Over the years our teams have brought home medals in soccer, basketball, and swimming —but our greatest victories are the personal growth, Jewish pride, and friendships that last.

A tradition of medals

More than three decades of competing with sportsmanship, representing our community on every court, field, and pool.

30+ athletes bound for Austin

This year we aim to send at least 30 young athletes from our community to represent North Miami Beach at the Games.

Estimated from $2,500 per athlete

Estimated cost, subject to change: $2,500 for JCC members, $2,700 for non-members — covering registration, transportation, accommodations, uniforms, and more. Many families rely on community support.
