Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate?
The Games are for Jewish teen athletes ages 13–17. NMB athletes represent the North Miami Beach / MARJCC community — our team confirms each athlete's eligibility and division.
When and where are the Games?
The JCC Maccabi Games 2027 take place July 25–30, 2027 in Austin, Texas — Austin's third time hosting.
How much does it cost?
Estimated $2,500 for JCC members and $2,700 for non-members (subject to change). This covers registration, transportation, accommodations, uniforms and more. Many families rely on sponsorships and community support — reach out if cost is a barrier.
Which sports can my athlete join?
This year NMB fields Soccer, Basketball, Tennis, Swimming and Football, across several age divisions. Choose a division on the interest form.
Is this the official registration?
Not yet — the form on this site is an interest form. Official registration and payment open later. Filling it out now helps us plan and hold your athlete's spot.
Where do athletes stay during the Games?
JCC Maccabi athletes are traditionally hosted by local Jewish families in the host city, as part of the experience. Full housing details are shared with confirmed athletes.
How can I help if my child isn't competing?
You can sponsor the team or sponsor an athlete — every contribution helps send a young athlete to Austin.
Who do I contact?
Registration & program: Tomas Glasermann, Head of Delegation — tomasg@marjcc.org. Sponsorship: Roxana Markus — roxanam@marjcc.org.
