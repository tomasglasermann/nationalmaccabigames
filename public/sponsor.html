Sponsorship opportunities

Sponsor the team

The Maccabi Games are a unique opportunity to connect with thousands of families —not only from North Miami Beach, but from across North America and beyond. Your support gives your brand valuable exposure and, above all, sends our young athletes to the Games. 100% of your contribution is utilized by our agency in furtherance of our mission.

30+ athletes. One team. One shot at Austin.

Team NMB is sending 30+ Jewish teen athletes (ages 13–17) from North Miami Beach to the JCC Maccabi Games in Austin. Your sponsorship doesn't just put a logo on a jersey — it helps a kid get there. Your support helps cover registration, travel, lodging, uniforms and training, so cost is never the reason an athlete stays home.
